# RupeeKit commercial editorial policy

Last reviewed: 3 September 2026

## Purpose

RupeeKit publishes educational calculators and financial explainers. Commercial relationships must never change the factual answer, calculator output, ranking order, or editorial conclusion a reader sees.

## Where commercial links are allowed

Commercial links may appear only where the user already has a clear commercial intent, such as a broker comparison or a product-selection guide. They must not be injected into unrelated calculators, tax explainers, government-scheme pages, or informational updates merely because an affiliate programme exists.

## Disclosure rule

A clear disclosure must appear before the first commercial or affiliate link. The disclosure must state that RupeeKit may receive a referral or affiliate fee and that the commercial relationship does not determine comparison order, factual conclusions, or recommendations.

Affiliate/referral links must use `rel="sponsored"` together with appropriate security attributes for external links.

## Recommendation ordering

Recommendations are never ordered by commission, referral payout, campaign bonus, or commercial relationship. If RupeeKit cannot explain a ranking or "best for" label using user-relevant criteria that would remain valid without a payout, the ranking should not be published.

## Commercial claims and sources

Every commercial claim that can change — brokerage, account-maintenance charge, processing fee, premium, interest rate, eligibility threshold, product price, free period, or similar — must have:

1. a primary source from the provider or regulator;
2. a visible verification date near the comparison or claim; and
3. wording that tells readers to re-check the official source before acting.

Secondary comparison sites may be used to discover a change but not as the final source for a commercial claim.

## Review cadence

Commercial claims are reviewed at least every 30 days while a page carries active affiliate/referral links, and immediately when a provider announces a pricing or terms change. A claim that cannot be re-verified should be removed or clearly marked unavailable rather than carried forward from an old comparison.

High-risk claims such as brokerage schedules, annual-maintenance charges, loan pricing, insurance premiums, and regulatory eligibility must be rechecked before any paid promotion or campaign refresh.

## Sponsored placements

Sponsored placements are not accepted until RupeeKit reaches the threshold recorded in the monetisation-readiness decision. If introduced later, sponsorship must be visibly labelled and must not buy editorial ranking, schema ratings, reviews, or "best" status.

## Advertising

Display advertising is not currently enabled. Any later ad implementation must reserve layout space before loading, avoid intrusive interstitials, preserve mobile usability, and be checked against the current Core Web Vitals baseline before launch.

## Paid products

A paid RupeeKit product must sell a genuine feature or service, not access to information that is presented as an independent editorial recommendation. Paid access must not change the numerical result of a public calculator in order to upsell the user.

## Prohibited practices

RupeeKit does not:

- fabricate ratings, reviews, testimonials, or review schema;
- guarantee investment returns, loan approval, tax savings, insurance outcomes, or government benefits;
- order recommendations by payout;
- hide disclosures in a footer after the commercial link;
- send user-entered calculator values to an affiliate as hidden tracking data; or
- publish stale commercial pricing without a verification date.
