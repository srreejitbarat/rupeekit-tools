# SEO, answer quality and editorial audit

Scope: every route in lib/i18n/public-routes.json in English, Hindi and Bengali, plus shared source copy and translation catalogs. Work stays local.

1. Record a page-by-page baseline of HTTP status, title, description, H1, canonical, language alternates, structured data, translation leakage and editorial warning signs.
2. Review repeated copy, machine-oriented headings, broken translation markers, missing explanations and misleading localization. Style flags are review leads, not evidence of AI authorship.
3. Rewrite supported examples in plain language, keeping calculations, factual claims, sources and dates intact. Update corresponding translations when English keys change.
4. Repair systemic rendering/translation issues and add useful checks. Keep incomplete-translation release gates in place.
5. Rerun the audit and relevant checks. Deliver a per-page report and explicit remaining limitations; do not imply a factual or native-language review of every sentence.

Reference: Google's people-first content guidance and guidance on AI-generated content. The goal is useful, accurate answers for readers, not a claimed AI-detection score or ranking guarantee.
